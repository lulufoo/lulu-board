Questions, answered

Do I need an account?

No. “Start free” opens a blank board that is saved in the page URL. Sign in only if you want cloud boards and share links.

Where is my board stored?

Local boards are encoded in the URL after the #. Signed-in boards are saved to your account and open with a #b: link.

Can I share a board?

Yes. Save the board to the cloud, then create a share link. Anyone with the link can view it; signed-in people can edit when access is set to Edit.

Can I use it with my AI agent?

Yes. Lulu Board can authorize your agent to read and write your boards through an OAuth consent screen. AI writes the protocol; you keep editing on the canvas.

Where can I see this page again?

From the workspace, use “Product intro” in the side rail. The Onboarding button still opens the example board.
